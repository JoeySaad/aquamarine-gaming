How to Play

Penalty Rush

  • Tournament: pick a country, alternate shooting and saving, and win best-of-5 shootouts.
  • Advance through the quarterfinal, semifinal, and final to win the cup.
  • Shooting: move the crosshair, hold click to charge power, and release to shoot.
  • Medium power is most accurate. High power is stronger but riskier.
  • Saving: move the gloves, then click or press space to dive after the shot starts.
  • Classic: score as many penalties as possible before 3 fails.
