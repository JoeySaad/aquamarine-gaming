Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Aquamarine Gaming respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use aquamarinegaming.com and how that information may be used.

Information We Collect

Aquamarine Gaming does not require users to create an account to play games. We do not ask for personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or payment information to use the website.

However, some basic information may be collected automatically when you visit the site, such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Game pages opened
  • Device type
  • Browser type
  • Approximate region or country
  • Referring websites or links
  • Basic usage and performance data

Analytics

Aquamarine Gaming may use analytics tools, such as Vercel Analytics or similar services, to understand how visitors use the website. This helps us see which games are popular, fix problems, improve page performance, and make the website better.

Analytics data is used in an aggregated way and is not used to personally identify individual visitors.

Advertising

Aquamarine Gaming may display advertisements in the future. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent abuse.

If ads are added to the website, this Privacy Policy may be updated with more details about the advertising services used.

Cookies

Aquamarine Gaming may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site functionality, analytics, performance, and advertising if ads are enabled.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

Game Data

Some games may store basic progress, settings, scores, or preferences locally on your device using browser storage. This information is used to improve gameplay and may allow features such as saving progress, remembering settings, or tracking high scores.

Local game data is stored on your device and can usually be cleared through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

Aquamarine Gaming is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Aquamarine Gaming is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites.

Data Security

We try to keep the website safe and reliable, but no website or online service can guarantee complete security. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through the website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of the page will be changed.
